# PASADHI research releases

Research versions are per document series and are independent of application versions.

## Fireworks Full White Paper v1.1 — 28 October 2026

- Adds nine supplied serverless exports: 22 phases, 11,101 new logical requests.
- Retains the v1.0 cohort, producing 37 run/phase IDs and 11,221 unique requests.
- Adds load scaling, model screening, rejection analysis and error-timing coverage.
- Separates successful output rate from attempted/completed request rate.
- Documents short inputs, retry amplification, heuristic provider-reach flags,
  missing finish reasons, missing exact load driver and unverified billing estimates.
- Publishes an 11-page two-column PDF and editable Word document with 12 figures
  and 12 tables. Source fingerprints and delivery hashes accompany the report.
- No new inference, training or deployment was run to produce this revision.

## Fireworks Full White Paper v1.0 — 28 October 2026

Historical baseline: 15 runs and 120 canonical requests. Separates live deployment
outputs, local prompt-validation failures and deterministic simulation. The v1.0
PDF, Word document, manuscript and analysis are retained unchanged.

## Other paper series

The solution paper, full architecture paper and Docker fleet benchmark retain
their existing names and versions. They are distinct evidence sets; their version
numbers do not imply a Fireworks evaluation revision.
